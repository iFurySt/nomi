# Nomi iOS 1.0.2

## 简体中文

- 点按 LaTeX 公式即可在键盘上方编辑，边写边看排版结果。正文会高亮正在编辑的公式，独立公式上下也有了更均匀的留白。
- 选中文字，键盘工具栏会自动显示粗体、斜体等常用样式。点 Aa 打开「格式」面板，可以连续选取不同段落并设置格式。
- 改善了 iPhone Duo 折叠、展开和旋转时的工具栏布局，修复了目录向右拖动时脱离屏幕边缘的问题。
- 修复了从其他 App 切回时文稿偶尔显示空白，以及编辑列表文字时意外换行的问题。

## English

- Tap a LaTeX equation to edit it above the keyboard and see the result as you type. The equation stays highlighted in your note, and display equations have more even spacing above and below.
- Select text to bring up bold, italic, and other styles in the keyboard toolbar. Tap Aa to keep the Format panel open while you style different passages.
- Floating controls now sit more consistently as you fold, unfold, or rotate iPhone Duo. We also fixed an issue that let the sidebar slide away from the screen edge.
- Fixed an occasional blank note when returning from another app and unexpected line breaks when editing text in a list.
