Privacy policy · 隐私政策

Effective November 23, 2026 · 生效日期 2026 年 11 月 23 日

Notes on your device · 本机文稿

You can use Nomi without signing in. Local manuscripts are stored in the app’s own directory. Signing in enables a separate cloud library. Existing local manuscripts stay local unless you choose to transfer their content. 本机文稿可离线使用，登录后另外提供云端文稿库。

Accounts and sync · 账号与同步

When you sign in, Nomi processes your email, account and provider identifiers, and an app-generated device ID to authenticate your session. Password accounts use password hashes. Apple and Google process sign-in requests if you choose their buttons. Nomi stores session credentials in the system keychain.

The sync service stores the titles, contents, revisions and attachment data of cloud manuscripts to make them available to your authorized devices. Operational logs record request type, result and timing. Hosting and network providers process traffic needed to deliver this service. Nomi does not use your manuscripts for advertising or track you across apps.

我们用邮箱、登录身份和设备会话标识提供认证，并保存云端文稿、版本及附件以完成同步。本应用不通过文稿投放广告或跨应用跟踪。

Sharing and control · 分享与删除

You choose when to share a manuscript through the system share sheet. Signing out ends the session on the device. To permanently delete your account, open the directory, choose your profile, select 永久注销账号, and confirm. Account deletion removes cloud manuscripts, revision history, account records and sessions. Attachment cleanup is retried if interrupted. Copies you shared, independent local notes and downloads on other devices may remain. Restricted recovery backups may retain earlier records until the backups are retired.

从目录打开个人设置，点「永久注销账号」并确认可删除账号和云端资料。恢复备份中的历史数据可能保留到该备份退役。其他设备已下载的副本和独立本机文稿可在对应设备管理。

If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also stop using Nomi in Apple Account under Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple. 如曾通过 Apple 登录，也可在 Apple 账户中管理授权。
