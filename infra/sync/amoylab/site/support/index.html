Nomi support · 使用支持

Nomi is a manuscript editor for local notes and optional account sync. Nomi 支持离线写作和登录后的多设备同步。

Get help · 联系开发者

Email ifuryst@gmail.com for account, sync or privacy requests. For a non-private bug report, visit Nomi on GitHub. Include the app version, device model and steps to reproduce. Please omit passwords, sign-in codes and private manuscripts.

遇到账号或同步问题可直接发邮件。请附版本号、设备型号和操作步骤，并避免发送密码、验证码或私人文稿。

Manage an account · 账号管理

Open the manuscript directory and tap your profile. From there you can sign out or permanently delete the account and its cloud manuscripts. 目录 → 个人设置 → 永久注销账号。仅希望结束当前设备的会话时，选择「退出登录」。
